## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original game homepage illustration for a cross-platform multiplayer board-game app, reusable in a wide game-library tile and a game entrance.
Primary request: a richly tactile 3D miniature of an underground gold mine, conveying digging connected tunnels and discovering gold. An open cutaway through a thick irregular slab of dark rock reveals branching mine passages with worn timber supports; a small iron pickaxe, an old brass lantern and a few gold nuggets make the scale and purpose immediately legible.
Style/medium: art-directed miniature-diorama render with hand-crafted realism, sculpted chipped stone, wood grain, scratched metal, convincing thickness and soft contact shadows. Sophisticated game artwork, not a flat vector or a plastic toy.
Composition/framing: one cohesive landscape composition, roughly 3:2, central subject comfortably inside the middle 80% for responsive cropping. Three-quarter elevated camera. The scene fills the frame with clear large silhouettes that remain legible at 300 pixels wide. No UI or border frame.
Lighting/mood: warm lantern and upper-left key light, deep aubergine shadows, gold highlights, believable bounce light and depth. Dark purple-brown ground fits the existing mine game palette (#130E1F, #241B36, #F5C518) without neon glow.
Constraints: entirely original illustrative scene, not a reproduction of any published game box or cards. No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, people or character faces. Do not draw playing cards, game-state diagrams or a complete game board. No flat icon treatment, no outlines, no exaggerated glossy plastic, no UI mockup.
