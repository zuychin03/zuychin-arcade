## Shared exact prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: one original square loot-art vignette for a mobile-first sky-pirate tabletop game.
Style/medium: premium photographed handcrafted fantasy game prop, tangible carved timber, worn cloth, aged brass and ceramic detail, physical contact shadows and restrained imperfections. A single clearly identifiable object, not flat vector art, a UI or a full card.
Composition/framing: compact centred still life on a low navy timber stand, three-quarter product view, complete primary object inside a generous square margin, readable at 96 pixels.
Lighting/palette: dark navy #080C1C backdrop, soft sky-cyan #6DE1FF rim, restrained muted violet #B18CFF and warm gold #F5C85B detail. Airy adventurous sky-fleet identity, not a horror sea voyage. No neon or excessive bloom.
Constraints: original object, no commercial game artwork, printed words, numbers, letters, logos, watermark, card border, ranks, score values, game effect symbols, people or recognisable franchise design. Depicts a loot kind only; exact calm-side rules stay in live interface text.

## Exact subject prompts

### map

Primary request: an open small weathered parchment chart, curled edges held by a plain brass weight, with faint illustrative floating-island silhouettes but absolutely no letters, words, numerals, compass labels, route lines or gameplay positions. Parchment itself is the distinctive central object.

### barrel

Primary request: one compact miniature oak barrel with aged brass hoops and a closed lid, a tiny blue cloth tied loosely around one handle. Distinct upright rounded silhouette, no spilled contents or markings.

### amulet

Primary request: one elegant brass pendant amulet with a faceted pale-cyan stone and a short loosely coiled dark chain, resting on folded muted-violet cloth. Simple strong jewel silhouette, no engraved symbols, eye, runes or numbers.

### chest

Primary request: a small handcrafted sky-pirate treasure chest of dark wood and aged brass, lid slightly open to show a quiet warm glow and a few indistinct plain metal pieces. No exact visible currency count, lettering or lock emblem.

### hook

Primary request: one classic curved polished-brass pirate hook fixed to a worn dark leather cuff, resting diagonally on the navy stand. Clearly a wearable hook prop rather than an anchor or sharp weapon in use; no limb, person or injury.

### saber

Primary request: one small original curved sabre prop with an aged-brass guard, dark grip and a restrained bright blade, lying diagonally on a folded sky-blue cloth. Full blade and hilt visible, elegant miniature not a real product model; no people or violence.

### relic

Primary request: one ominous sealed dark-violet stone relic, an irregular carved shard bound by aged-brass bands, a dim violet fissure and a tiny wisp of cool shadow. It should feel risky and mysterious, distinct from positive bright jewellery. No skull, face, runes, words or explicit game-value symbols.
