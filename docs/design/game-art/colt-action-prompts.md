# Colt Express action vignettes

Historical image-generation and asset-validation record, 22/09/2026. Status statements below describe that preparation step, not the current delivery checklist. Production components define current integration; see [the design guide](../../../DESIGN.md) and [testing limits](../../TESTING.md).

Built-in imagegen, 22/09/2026 (Australia/Sydney). Seven original decorative action identities. Rules, ownership and timing stay live in the component; concealed programme cards must not select an action image. Source and optimised-output hashes are recorded in the generated manifests. Application acceptance is separate from image generation.

Sources are `colt-action-{move,floor,rob,shoot,punch,marshal,bullet}.png`, each 1254×1254. Root inspected all original outputs and all seven optimised 320×320 WebPs. The assets total 100,178 bytes, below the 224 KiB collection budget. Generation preserves the complete source composition; there is no crop or padding. Eight asset tests and deterministic regeneration checks pass. Manifests live under `apps/mobile/assets/game-art/colt-action-*-manifest.json`.

## move

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: one original square action-card art vignette for a tactile train-robbery tabletop app, displayed as a compact illustration on phone and desktop cards.
Primary request: A pair of worn dark leather Western boots on a small timber-and-rail base, one boot stepping forward. Strong readable walking silhouette, no person or legs.
Style/medium: convincing three-dimensional handcrafted miniature photographed close-up, beautifully tactile and slightly stylised. Not a flat icon, not vector clipart and not glossy plastic. All related action vignettes belong to a single warm Western miniature collection.
Composition: square image, a single tightly grouped subject centred and fully visible with approximately 10 percent clear padding at every edge. Elevated three-quarter viewpoint. Large simple silhouette legible at 100 pixels wide, with uncluttered near-black charcoal background #0C0D12. Background meets the edges without a border or frame.
Materials/light: directional warm amber light from upper left, gentle cool fill, natural contact shadows, textured leather or weathered timber, charcoal iron and softly worn brass. Limited colours charcoal #171A24, ember #FF8A48, brass #F4C458, small warm ivory highlights. No glowing outlines, no bloom, no fog.
Constraints: no text, letters, numbers, logos, UI, card frame or watermark. Original props only, no copied publisher artwork or named characters. Image is decorative action identity only, not a rules diagram or actual hidden game state. Keep the whole main object inside the frame.
