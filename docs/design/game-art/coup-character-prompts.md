# Coup character vignettes

Historical image-generation and asset-validation record, 22/09/2026. Status statements below describe that preparation step, not the current delivery checklist. Production components define current integration; see [the design guide](../../../DESIGN.md) and [testing limits](../../TESTING.md).

Built-in imagegen, 22/09/2026 (Australia/Sydney). Preproduction for six original role illustrations; no application acceptance implied. Names and rules remain live, and face-down cards never select role artwork.

Sources are `coup-character-{duke,assassin,captain,ambassador,contessa,inquisitor}.png`, each 1254×1254. All six source outputs and optimised 320×320 WebPs were personally inspected. The role collection totals 58,330 bytes, below its 192 KiB budget. Nine asset tests, scoped lint and deterministic regeneration checks pass. Exact source/output hashes are in `apps/mobile/assets/game-art/coup-character-*-manifest.json`; no crop or padding is applied.

## duke

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original square character vignette for a physical-looking influence card in a phone-first bluffing tabletop app.
Primary request: A composed older court ruler, a distinctive short beard, sculpted angular face, deep purple velvet mantle and a small understated brass coronet. Strong regal silhouette, calm expression.
Style: a single exquisitely crafted and hand-painted miniature character bust, head and upper torso with a small dark stone plinth. Sculptural three-dimensional anatomy, convincing fine cloth folds, subtly worn brass and painted matte resin. Not a real-person portrait, not flat vector artwork, not a generic icon. All six court characters should read as one cohesive miniature collection.
Composition: square, straight-on with a slight three-quarter turn, large face and upper torso occupy most of the image with complete head, shoulder edges, props and plinth visible. At least 8 percent empty margin. Simple solid deep wine near-black #140A12 background, no room scene. Character must remain recognisable at 90 pixels wide.
Lighting: soft warm directional light from upper left and a quiet cool rim, honest contact shadow below the plinth, rich role-coloured cloth with warm neutral skin and worn brass accents. No glow or bright bloom.
Constraints: wholly original character, no copyrighted publisher portraits, no resemblance to an actor or real person, no text, numbers, logos, watermark, card borders or UI. The illustration conveys identity only; live role name and rules will be outside it. No weapon handling instructions, no blood, no sexualisation.
